When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Shoreline

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
